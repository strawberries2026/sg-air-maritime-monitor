Observation time and limitations

These links open the providers' own viewers. Select a layer and date there to see its observation timestamp. Imagery is not continuous live video; cloud cover, revisit times, and processing delays affect availability.

Satellite imagery does not directly identify or continuously track aircraft or vessels. The existing map uses separate ADS-B/AIS feeds with partial coverage.

No satellite imagery is stored by this website. External providers may have separate privacy and usage policies. This is a supplementary public-information reference, not for operational decisions.
