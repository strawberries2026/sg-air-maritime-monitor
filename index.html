How are these figures calculated?

Observed counts are distinct identifiers in the published position history within the past 24 hours, not arrivals, departures, or total traffic. Coverage is partial. Crossing counts require consecutive valid observations on opposite sides of an agreed boundary, direction checks, and deduplication; they are intentionally unavailable until validated. Military air bases are not included in live movement counts.
